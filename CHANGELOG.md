## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22

First public release.

### Added
- Full-screen wallpaper with a frosted-glass greeting, clock and date, and a glass dock.
- Continue watching row for the focused dock app (where the TV allows apps to read it).
- Nyumbani settings: your name, greeting (time of day or custom), location, 12/24-hour clock,
  dock apps (up to 5, reorderable), wallpaper, Home screen setup, Android TV settings and About.
- First-run setup: name, location and dock apps; every step can be skipped.
- Hold OK on a dock app to move it left or right, or remove it.
- Four bundled wallpapers, plus your own: send a photo from your phone by scanning a QR code,
  or copy one from a USB drive.
- "Make Nyumbani your Home screen" asks the TV directly where it can, with ADB steps otherwise.
- Update check in About and once a day in the background (a dot on the gear); download and
  install from the TV, checked against the release's SHA-256 checksums.
